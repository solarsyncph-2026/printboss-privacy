PrintBoss Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026.

PrintBoss ("we", "us") is operated by AYA DIGITAL PRINTING SERVICES, Philippines. This policy explains what information the PrintBoss website (printboss.pro) and Android app collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Contact: appprintboss@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information. Your name, email address, and sign-in details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.

Shop information you enter. Shop name, address, contact number, products and prices, job orders, quotations, customer records (names, phone numbers, addresses, social profiles), inventory, expenses and payment records. You control this information; we store it so the service can work.

Messages. If you connect a Facebook Page, we receive and store the Messenger conversations between your Page and your customers so you can read and reply in PrintBoss. Connecting a Page is optional and you can disconnect it at any time.

Photos and files. Images you upload (for example product photos and chat attachments) are stored with your account. Photos you edit in Photo Studio are processed on your device and are not uploaded unless you choose to save or send them.

Usage and diagnostics. We use PostHog to understand how features are used and to find errors. This includes pages and buttons used, device and browser type, and error reports, linked to your account. In the Android app, Google Firebase Crashlytics also collects crash reports (app version, device model, Android version and the error details). Crash reports are not linked to your account.

Notifications. If you allow notifications in the Android app, we store a device token so we can send you alerts (for example new quote requests). You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

Purchases. If you subscribe to PRO in the Android app, Google Play handles the payment; we receive the order ID, the plan and its renewal status from Google so we can turn PRO on. We never see your card, GCash or other payment details.

How we use information

  • To provide and operate PrintBoss for your shop (orders, quotations, inventory, finances, inbox).
  • To send notifications you have allowed.
  • To provide support and keep the service secure.
  • To improve features using aggregated usage information.

We do not sell your data or your customers' data, and we do not use your client lists or pricing for our own marketing.

Service providers

We rely on providers that process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in), PostHog (analytics), Google Firebase (push notifications and crash reports), Google (sign-in), and Meta (Facebook Messenger, only if you connect a Page). Data may be processed outside the Philippines by these providers.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to each shop's records is limited to that shop's accounts.

Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, your shop data, customers, orders, messages and profile are deleted. Limited payment records may be kept where needed for accounting, without being linked to your account. Uploaded files (such as product photos and chat attachments) are removed on request — email us after deleting your account.

Deleting your account

  • In the app or website: Settings → Danger Zone → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and shop data.
  • By email: send a deletion request from your account email to appprintboss@gmail.com. We will process it within 30 days.

Children

PrintBoss is a business tool for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact appprintboss@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the "Last updated" date.
